# Ads and keyword strategy for executive AI demand

Fast Forward Labs should focus paid search on executive-intent queries where the buyer is already trying to solve an organizational AI problem.

## Primary keyword clusters

| Cluster | Example queries | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Executive AI education | AI for executives, executive AI course, AI training for leaders | High-intent upskilling demand |
| AI strategy | AI strategy for business, generative AI strategy, AI transformation consulting | Signals leadership planning and budget exploration |
| Implementation | c-suite AI implementation, AI adoption framework, agentic AI for enterprise | Aligns with outcome-driven consulting needs |
| ROI and governance | AI ROI framework, AI governance for executives, AI operating model | Captures boardroom-level urgency |

## Campaign structure

Use tightly grouped campaigns instead of one broad AI bucket:

1. Executive education and enablement
2. AI strategy and transformation
3. AI implementation and operating model
4. GEO / AI visibility advisory

## Negative keyword direction

Filter out low-intent consumer and hobbyist demand such as:

- free prompt list
- ChatGPT jailbreak
- student AI homework
- beginner AI coding tutorial
- image generator entertainment traffic

## Offer alignment

The paid search CTA should lead to executive-grade assets, not generic newsletters. Priority offers:

- AI strategy briefings
- executive readiness assessments
- operating model workshops
- GEO visibility audits

## Measurement

Track for quality over volume:

- cost per qualified executive lead
- meetings booked with leadership teams
- conversion rate by title and company size
- influenced pipeline from executive-intent keywords
