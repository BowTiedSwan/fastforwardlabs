# LinkedIn outreach and thought leadership strategy

LinkedIn is the highest-leverage platform for Fast Forward Labs because executive buyers are already in a professional evaluation context when they encounter AI strategy content there.

## Who to target

Primary roles:

- CEO
- CTO
- CIO
- CMO
- CFO
- transformation, strategy, and innovation leaders

## Organic content angles

The content mix should stay close to executive pain points:

| Angle | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| AI readiness gaps | Create urgency and shared recognition |
| ROI frameworks | Make the advisory offer concrete |
| Operating model clarity | Differentiate from generic AI hype |
| GEO and AI search visibility | Connect content strategy to executive outcomes |

## Outreach structure

Outreach should feel like peer-level diagnosis, not volume prospecting.

1. Start with a specific observation about market pressure or organizational friction.
2. Tie it to a concrete executive problem such as ROI ambiguity or cross-functional misalignment.
3. Offer a useful next step such as a briefing, audit, or strategy session.

## Message principles

- Lead with business consequence, not tooling novelty.
- Keep copy concise and direct.
- Use evidence-backed positioning instead of hype.
- Prefer relevance over scale.

## Supporting assets

The strongest LinkedIn outreach performs better when paired with:

- a sharp landing page
- an executive briefing article
- a GEO or readiness audit offer
- short-form proof points from market research and delivery work
