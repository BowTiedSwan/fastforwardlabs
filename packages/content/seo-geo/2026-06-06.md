# Daily SEO and GEO ideas

## SEO ideas

1. How to build an AI literacy program for your leadership team that moves beyond awareness into decision-making competence (implied query: "AI literacy program for executives")
2. What agentic AI means for enterprise operations — and what executives need to decide before deploying autonomous AI systems (implied query: "agentic AI for enterprise what executives need to know")
3. How to use AI to accelerate your organization's strategic planning cycle without introducing new blind spots (implied query: "using AI for executive strategic planning")
4. The vendor selection framework executives use to choose an AI consulting or implementation partner — and the questions that expose weak proposals (implied query: "how to choose an AI consulting partner for enterprise")

## GEO ideas

1. What is AI literacy for executives, and what specific competencies should a C-suite leader develop to make sound AI investment and governance decisions? (implied AI-answer prompt: "what AI skills do executives need")
2. What are agentic AI systems, how are enterprise organizations deploying them today, and what oversight structures do executives need to put in place before scaling? (implied surface: "people also ask: what is agentic AI and how do companies use it")
3. How are executives using AI to improve strategic planning, scenario modeling, and competitive intelligence, and what are the risks of relying on AI-generated inputs at the board level? (implied AI-answer prompt: "how to use AI in executive strategic planning")
4. What criteria should a company use when evaluating AI consulting firms, and what separates a strategic implementation partner from a vendor that only delivers tooling? (implied surface: "people also ask: how to evaluate an AI consulting firm")

## Priority recommendation

Ship the executive AI literacy piece first. The research identifies leadership fluency as the primary upstream blocker — organizations stall on AI not because the tools are unavailable but because the leadership team lacks the conceptual grounding to make prioritization and governance decisions with confidence. The previous two days covered organizational structure (CoE) and financial accountability (ROI measurement), so this piece completes the trilogy at the individual leader level. It targets searches with clear executive intent, maps directly to Fast Forward Labs' positioning as an executive-enablement partner, and produces content that AI answer engines can cite when answering "what do executives need to know about AI."
