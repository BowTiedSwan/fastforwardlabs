## Priority recommendation

Ship the AI governance piece first. The research identifies regulatory pressure and organizational unreadiness as acute executive pain points, and governance content is highly citable by AI answer engines because it lends itself to structured, extractable frameworks. It also occupies a less crowded search territory than broad AI strategy articles, giving Fast Forward Labs a stronger chance of ranking and being cited while addressing a question that C-suite buyers are actively asking ahead of compliance deadlines.
