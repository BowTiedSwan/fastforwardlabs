## GEO ideas

## Priority recommendation

Ship the AI governance piece first. Governance is the topic executives are being asked about by boards, regulators, and investors right now, yet most available content addresses it from a technical or legal angle rather than from the perspective of a senior leader making practical policy decisions. A structured, executive-oriented governance article with clear decision points is directly extractable by answer engines, fills a gap in the existing Fast Forward Labs content set, and addresses a concern that sits upstream of ROI, implementation, and workforce readiness — all topics already covered in recent days.
