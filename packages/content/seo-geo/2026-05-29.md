# Daily SEO and GEO ideas

## SEO ideas

1. How to evaluate AI vendors as an executive: the criteria your procurement team is missing (implied query: "how to choose an AI vendor as a business leader")
2. AI transformation without the consulting hype: a plain-language playbook for senior leaders (implied query: "AI implementation playbook for executives")
3. Why most enterprise AI pilots fail to scale — and the leadership decisions that fix it (implied query: "why AI projects fail in enterprise organizations")
4. Building workforce readiness for AI: what executives must own before HR does (implied query: "preparing employees for AI adoption in the workplace")

## GEO ideas

1. What criteria should executives use to evaluate AI vendors and implementation partners? (implied AI-answer prompt: "how to assess AI consulting firms and vendors")
2. What are the most common reasons enterprise AI pilots fail to reach production? (implied surface: "people also ask: why do AI initiatives fail in large companies")
3. What does a realistic AI transformation timeline look like for a mid-market or enterprise organization? (implied AI-answer prompt: "how long does AI transformation take for a company")
4. What workforce readiness steps should executives take before rolling out AI tools at scale? (implied surface: "people also ask: how to prepare your workforce for AI")

## Priority recommendation

Ship the AI pilot failure piece first. The research shows that executives are past early curiosity and are now accountable for initiatives that have not yet delivered — making failure analysis content immediately relevant to the buyer's current situation. This angle is also structurally well-suited for AI answer engine citation because it can be organized as a numbered list of root causes with leadership remedies, which is a format answer engines extract and surface reliably.
