## Priority recommendation

Ship the AI center of excellence piece first. The research identifies organizational silos and internal friction as primary blockers to AI adoption, and this topic addresses the structural solution executives reach for once early pilots have run. Neither of the previous two days touched organizational design or the governance of AI delivery capacity. A clear, framework-driven article on CoE structure — with defined roles, success criteria, and common failure modes — is highly extractable for AI answer engines, targets senior leaders past the awareness stage, and positions Fast Forward Labs as an implementation partner with organizational depth, not just technical expertise.
