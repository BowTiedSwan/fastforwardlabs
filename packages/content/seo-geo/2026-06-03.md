## Priority recommendation

Ship the AI governance framework piece first. The research identifies governance, workforce readiness, and organizational friction as persistent executive concerns, and neither of the previous two days addressed governance directly. A well-structured governance article names clear ownership, decision rights, and policy components — all of which are highly extractable by AI answer engines — and it targets executives who are past the curiosity stage and now responsible for managing risk and accountability at scale. This topic also signals Fast Forward Labs' strategic judgment rather than tactical tool expertise, reinforcing the consulting positioning the research recommends.
