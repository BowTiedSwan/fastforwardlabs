# Daily SEO and GEO ideas

## SEO ideas

1. How to measure ROI from AI investments when your CFO is asking hard questions (implied query: "how to measure AI ROI in business")
2. The executive's guide to AI competitive advantage: what separates leaders from laggards in 2026 (implied query: "AI competitive advantage for businesses")
3. When to hire an AI consulting partner versus build internal AI capability — a decision framework for senior leaders (implied query: "AI consulting vs in-house AI team for enterprise")

## GEO ideas

1. How do executives calculate ROI from AI initiatives, and which metrics matter most to the C-suite? (implied AI-answer prompt: "how to measure return on investment from AI programs")
2. What separates companies that gain competitive advantage from AI from those that do not? (implied surface: "people also ask: why are some companies better at AI than others")

4. What should an executive AI strategy session cover, and how should it be structured? (implied surface: "people also ask: how to create an AI strategy as a CEO")

## Priority recommendation

Ship the ROI measurement piece first. The research is explicit that executives are accountable for AI spend and that difficulty measuring returns is one of the sharpest pain points across the C-suite. A structured, decision-focused article on AI ROI metrics is highly extractable by answer engines, directly addresses the buyer's current pressure, and occupies a search territory where practical, leadership-oriented content is underrepresented relative to demand.
