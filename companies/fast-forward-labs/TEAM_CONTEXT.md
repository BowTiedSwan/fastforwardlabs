# Fast Forward Labs — Shared Team Context

Fast Forward Labs is an AI consulting agency. Every agent on the team must read this file before producing any client-facing or prospect-facing output. It defines who we sell to, what we sell, how we position it, and the language we use.

## Service-as-software thesis

- AI replaces human labor inside the agency. One person does the work of seven.
- Gross margins jump from ~40% (traditional agency) to ~75% (service-as-software).
- Most mid-market executives can't keep up with AI themselves. The product is clarity translated to their business.
- Every vertical has 2–3 repeatable operational bottlenecks. Solutions are templatable after the first build.

## Offer stack

### Tier 1 — AI Clarity Briefing
- Price: $2,500 / month (recommend 6-month minimum)
- Capacity: 12–15 clients
- Target revenue: $30K–$37.5K / month
- Who: founders and CEOs at $2M–$30M revenue who can't ignore AI but have no plan
- What: 1× 60-min strategy call / month, monthly industry-tailored AI briefing, async access (48h), quarterly "State of AI for Your Industry" memo
- Not: implementation, generic advice
- Upgrade trigger: "okay, but who's going to DO this?" → Tier 2 or 3

### Tier 2 — 90-Day AI Transformation Roadmap
- Price: $5,000–$8,000 / month
- Commitment: 3 months
- Capacity: 5–7 active clients
- Target revenue: $25K–$56K / month
- Who: CEOs at $1M–$15M past "should we?" and into "how do we?"
- Phases: (1) Leader Fluency wks 1–4, (2) Operational Audit wks 2–6, (3) Roadmap wks 4–8, (4) Implementation Coaching wks 8–12
- Cadence: 2× strategy calls / month, direct async access
- Upgrade trigger: roadmap done, client asks for hands-on build → Tier 3

### Tier 3 — AI Operating System Build
- Price: $10,000–$20,000 / month
- Commitment: 3–6 months
- Capacity: 3–4 active clients
- Target revenue: $30K–$80K / month
- Who: companies $3M–$50M with a clear operational bottleneck
- What: custom AI system builds tied to vertical pain points, workflow automation, tool selection, team training, monthly ROI reviews
- Upgrade trigger: client wants ongoing embedded role → Tier 4

### Tier 4 — Fractional AI Leadership
- Price: $15,000–$30,000 / month
- Commitment: 6–12 months
- Capacity: 2–3 clients
- Target revenue: $30K–$90K / month
- Who: $5M–$150M companies where AI is becoming core to the operating model
- What: fractional Head of AI, leadership meetings, ongoing builds, vendor management, hiring support, board-level advisory
- Cadence: weekly strategy + implementation review

## Industry playbook (prospecting cheat sheet)

Every pitch leads with the operational problem, not "AI." The 25 verticals and their core bottleneck / solution:

Services & Professional
- Law firms — intake/voicemail/email sits for days → intake-to-assignment system
- Accounting firms — chasing 200+ clients every tax season → automated document-request workflows + status tracking
- Marketing agencies — 5–10 hrs/week pulling reports → automated dashboards with client-ready summaries
- Recruiting/staffing — manual resume review → AI screening + top-10% surfacing
- Architecture/engineering — scope creep, billing disputes → time tracking tied to project phases + change-order approval
- Financial advisors — inconsistent client comms → automated touchpoint cadence

Real Estate & Property
- Real estate brokerages — lead follow-up dies day 2 → nurture + AI follow-up + pipeline routing
- Property management — maintenance requests scattered → capture + urgency sort + auto-vendor assignment

Healthcare
- Medical/dental — no-shows, scheduling chaos → reminders + AI rescheduling + waitlist fill
- Multi-provider clinics — paper intake re-entered 3× → digital intake → provider systems + pre-populated notes

Commerce & Logistics
- E-commerce — tier-1 ticket volume → AI agents + proactive order tracking
- Logistics/freight — 4+ systems for quoting → centralized quoting engine
- Wholesale/distribution — orders via email/phone manually entered → automated order capture → fulfillment

Trades & Local Services
- Home services — missed calls = missed revenue → AI answering + appointment booking
- Commercial cleaning — manual scheduling + quality verification → GPS check-in + photo verification + client dashboards
- Auto dealerships — weak test-drive follow-up → multi-touch behavior-triggered sequences + AI lead scoring

Tech & Finance
- SaaS — churn signals unnoticed → churn prediction + retention triggers
- Private equity — deal flow scattered → deal pipeline OS with organized diligence
- Insurance — policy renewals lost → automated renewal pipeline + reminders + agent queueing

Other
- Construction — project updates scattered → project hub with milestone tracking + delay flagging
- Manufacturing — reactive QC → AI defect detection + real-time monitoring
- Restaurants/hospitality — manual inventory → AI forecasting from sales + seasonality + reservations
- Fitness studios — silent retention drop → engagement tracking + re-engagement flows
- Education/courses — poor completion rates → progress tracking + AI nudges at drop-off
- Nonprofits — donor chaos → donor CRM with automated acknowledgments + campaign dashboards

## Positioning rules (apply everywhere)

- Sell the outcome, not "AI." The problem is operational — broken handoffs, manual entry, disconnected systems, people-memory dependencies.
- Anchor price against cost of inaction: "one wrong AI tool purchase or wasted implementation easily costs more than a full year of advisory."
- Scarcity is real: limited client slots at each tier, state them honestly.
- "This is for you if / not for you if" blocks are the default way to filter prospects on sales pages.
- Credibility line: "I'm not advising from theory. I'm making these same restructuring decisions in real businesses, in real time."

## Target ICP (who agents are writing for)

- Primary persona: operator-CEO at $1M–$50M revenue, responsible for margins, feeling AI pressure, short on time, buys outcomes not frameworks.
- Secondary persona: COO or Head of Operations responsible for process bottlenecks.
- Tertiary persona: PE-backed portfolio company leader under margin-expansion mandate.

## Voice and tone

- Plain English. No jargon ("synergy," "leverage," "unlock"). No hedging ("potentially," "possibly").
- Concrete numbers > vague claims. Specific verticals > generic "businesses."
- Operator-to-operator voice. Never "as an AI" or "as a consultant."
- Frame in hours saved, leads recovered, margin points added, FTE equivalents replaced.

## Client journey (natural escalation)

Prospect → T1 Clarity Briefing → (3–6 mo later) → T2 Transformation Roadmap → T3 OS Build → T4 Fractional AI Leadership.

Agents should design output to fit where the prospect is in this path. Top-of-funnel (LinkedIn, SEO) targets T1 entry. Mid-funnel (CRO, copywriting, outbound) converts into T2. Bottom-funnel (case studies, strategy content) graduates into T3/T4.
